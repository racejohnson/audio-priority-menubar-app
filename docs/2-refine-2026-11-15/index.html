What changed since round 1

Panel: B · Quiet list only. No coloured badges; one system-blue check per list. Inset puts each list on a DayNight-style card.

Glyph: Capsule only, always two glyphs, so the status item never changes width.

Symbols: real SF Symbols everywhere; the DJI glyph is the one custom drawing.

Behaviour (ported from camguillory)

Headphones rank above speakers. A device you click stays until it disconnects or a higher-ranked one connects.

If AirPods or macOS take the output, it switches back. The microphone is held: AirPods connecting never take it from the DJI mic.

The Capsule

Output on the left, input on the right, in one template image (one ink, tinted by macOS). Three finishes, each shown at 3× and at real size in a light and dark menu bar beside real Wi-Fi, battery and Control Center symbols. With Auto off the capsule fills and the glyphs are cut out of it. The Input side switch above changes the right-hand glyph here too.

System glyphs

The app asks macOS for these by name, so they match Control Center and Sound settings and follow future redesigns. Shown at capsule size (10 pt semibold) and panel-row size (13 pt medium). The only custom glyph is the DJI Mic Mini.

DJI Mic Mini glyph

One transmitter at every size, built from your reference: a rounded-square body with three slots centred near the top. Proportions are measured from the reference's front unit (corner radius 27% of the width, slots 20% down). Each has two cuts, as SF Symbols do: regular for 16 pt and up, and a small cut with wider slots for the capsule.

Your reference. Two overlapping units, three slots each. You chose a single unit everywhere, so Solid is the front unit alone, Outline is the same body in the weight of laptopcomputer and mic, and Solid + clip keeps a sliver of the back unit as the magnetic clip.

App icon

Five new drawings that keep an audio-level feel without the five-bar equalizer. Same family construction as DayNight: 824 pt tile on a 1024 canvas, one bold glyph, a separate heavier drawing for 16 and 32 px, dark and tinted renditions. Mock-ups; the shipping icon is an Icon Composer document.
