Kept behaviour (all directions)

Headphones rank above speakers. Auto picks the first connected device in each list; a device you click stays until it disconnects or a higher-ranked one connects.

If AirPods or macOS take the output on their own, it switches back. The microphone is held: AirPods connecting never take it from the DJI mic.

Dropped: profiles, mute hotkey, Shortcuts, URL commands, switch notices, Jabra, update checker.

Menu-bar glyphs: output and input together

Six ways to show both devices in one status item. Each is a template image (one ink, tinted by macOS), shown at real size (1 pt = 1 px here) in a light and dark menu bar beside Wi-Fi, battery and Control Center, and zoomed 3× below. The dashed outline marks the item's width. Manual mode (Auto off) adds a dot, or fills the capsule. Device glyphs are drawn stand-ins for SF Symbols; the app uses the real ones.

DJI Mic Mini symbol

macOS lists the DJI receiver as a USB input named "Wireless Mic Rx", so a generic mic or USB glyph would hide it. These symbols draw the transmitter you clip on, on the SF Symbols 18 pt grid (red 1 pt grid, blue dashed 16 pt live area), at the weight of the built-in symbols beside them. References: DJI's spec page (TX 26.55 × 26.06 × 15.96 mm, a near-square face on a thick body; RX 46.50 × 29.61 × 19.32 mm) and memory of DJI's product renders: a rounded transmitter with a magnetic clip, an optional furry windscreen, and a receiver with a USB-C plug. I could read the page text but not its photos, so check the silhouettes against the real thing before one ships.

Optional: the receiver

The piece macOS actually sees. Too abstract to read as "my mic" in the menu bar, so it's offered only for the row's detail line or a Settings list.

App icon

Six takes on mateusbadalotti/audio-priority's blue equalizer, rebuilt in Race's family: an 824 pt Icon Composer tile on a 1024 canvas, one bold glyph, a separate heavier drawing for 16 and 32 px. Shown at 1024 (scaled), 128, 32 and 16 px, plus dark and tinted. These are mock-ups; the shipping icon is an Icon Composer document like DayNight's.

Beside the family

DayNight, MediaKeys for Spotify and Screenshot Pathname are their real 128 px icons; Polaris is mac-reach's chosen icon. Pick an option above to swap it in.
