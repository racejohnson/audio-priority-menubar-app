Peaks, simpler and bolder.

Five takes on the Peaks meter, held to the bar of Claude, Journal, Spotify, Time Machine and Tailscale: one big glyph, one or two colours, no fuss. Each is a shippable set (1024 master, 16/32 px cut, full-bleed art for macOS 26’s shape) that drops into Icon/build-icon.sh unchanged.

Every icon on this page is the SVG in svg/, inlined; the pixel views are rendered with rsvg-convert, the same renderer build-icon.sh uses. Rebuild with node docs/4-icon-elevate-2026-11-15/source/build.mjs.
